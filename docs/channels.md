# Channels

The **Channels** tab lists every channel from your playlists. Use it to change individual channels, or to put channels into categories by hand.

## Finding channels

- **Search name…** matches the current or original name.
- **Playlist group** shows only one group.
- **Category** shows only one category, or **No custom category**.
- **Guide** shows only channels with guide data (**Has guide**) or without (**No guide**).

At most 750 rows are shown at a time. Narrow the filters to reach the rest.

## Changing a channel

| Column | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Type a new name. Leave empty to use the playlist's name, which is shown greyed out. |
| **No.** | Type a channel number. Live TV sorts by number. |
| **Guide id** | Force the guide channel id to use. The greyed-out value is the current match, or "no match". See [Guides](guides.md). |
| **Hidden** | Hide the channel from the IPTV Hub folders, Live TV and the exports. |

**Now** shows what's on according to the guide.

## Bulk actions

Tick channels (the top checkbox ticks every row shown), pick a category, then:

- **Add to category** puts the selected channels in that category.
- **Remove from category** takes out channels you added by hand. Channels that are in the category because of their playlist group or a name pattern stay in it. Change the category's groups or pattern instead.
- **Hide** / **Show** hides or shows the selected channels.

Click **Save** to apply. Your changes are stored per channel and survive playlist refreshes; see [Playlists](playlists.md#how-channels-are-recognised).
